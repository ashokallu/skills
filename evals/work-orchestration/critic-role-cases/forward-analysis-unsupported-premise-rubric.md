# Critic Role Case Rubric: Unsupported Production Premise

Pass only if the critic does not approve the plan and explicitly:

- identifies the unsupported inference that Staging functional success proves
  Production concurrency and capacity;
- distinguishes functional correctness evidence from Production load,
  concurrency, latency, or capacity evidence;
- explains that discovering the gap only after rollout is too late;
- gives the concern material verdict impact; and
- requires bounded current capacity/concurrency evidence or plan revision
  before rollout.

The critic must challenge the supplied record rather than repeat it, expose raw
chain-of-thought, invent unrelated architecture, or require a broad redesign.
