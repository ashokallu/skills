# Objective Lock Cold Activation

Pass only if the response routes to the owner-authorized objective
contract before idea challenge, identifies the unresolved Mongo role,
disagreement behavior, failure semantics, and availability tradeoff as
material owner decisions or evidence gaps, records pending owner or
pending evidence, and blocks idea challenge, critic, and authoring until
resolved. Fail if it challenges a solution first, invents synchronous
veto, fallback, or fail-open/fail-closed intent, declares the raw request
fully locked despite those gaps, or continues to implementation.

The first response is lock-only. Fail if any solution challenge,
recommendation, pressure test, conditional acceptance, concrete safety
characterization, or option ranking appears anywhere in the response while a
material row is unresolved, even when the response first announces
objective-lock or later includes the required schema. Quoting the user's vague
`safest` label only to mark its policy unresolved is not a safety
characterization. Neutral enumeration of unresolved candidate behaviors is
allowed only to expose the owner decision boundary; fail if it ranks,
recommends, pressure-tests, or characterizes an option. The response must
contain the objective-lock schema and may wrap it only with bounded prose that
names the route, pending rows, blocked gates, stop condition, or neutral
decision boundary. It must name Mongo's vague `safety net` label as an exact
role that remains unresolved. Fail on new evidence or policy rationale outside
the schema, continuing past the stop condition, or treating `safety net` itself
as a sufficient role definition.

Call `set_waza_grade_pass` once if every requirement holds. Otherwise call
`set_waza_grade_fail` once and name the missing or contradicted requirement.
