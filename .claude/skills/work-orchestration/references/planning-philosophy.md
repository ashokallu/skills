# Planning Philosophy

```text
Type: companion contract
Use when: the earliest active planning gate must choose or challenge a substantive
          direction with credible alternatives, non-local effects, or material unknowns
Skip when: work is mechanical, fast-exit, or executing an accepted unchanged plan
Owns: bounded selection of one load-bearing mechanism, a credible rival, engineering
      translation, and reversal evidence inside the current planning decision
Does not own: routing, objectives, authorization, implementation, research, or a new output
Allowed side effects: none beyond bounded reads already allowed by the consuming owner
Inputs: locked objective or clear request, proposal, alternatives, evidence, consumers,
        protected invariants, and non-goals
Exit states: applied | no material influence | blocked by evidence or owner decision
Outputs: decision-changing claims placed in the consuming owner's existing fields
Re-enter when: objective, proposal, material evidence, consumer acceptance, or
               reversal condition changes
```

Use this companion to improve a planning decision, not to decorate it. A useful
principle changes an alternative, evidence request, acceptance condition,
prediction, or stop. Familiar names, proverbs, and metaphors carry no authority
by themselves.

This is not a lane, gate, checklist, acknowledgement ritual, public packet, or
request for hidden chain-of-thought. Keep the bounded conclusion and discard
private scratch work. Owner-controlled objectives, ethics, policy, invariants,
and non-goals remain authoritative. If a transfer would change one, return that
decision to `objective-lock` and the accountable owner.

Render retained effects in the consuming owner's requested output shape. When
the request calls for ordinary engineering language, give the engineering
decision without exposing this companion's router, steps, lens names, or
internal paths; this companion never creates a workflow packet.

## Activation And Single Consumption

Apply this companion when any of these can change a substantive planning
choice:

- more than one credible direction or a consequential no-op;
- a downstream consumer, shared resource, irreversible step, broad reach, or
  costly recovery;
- a feedback loop, delay, threshold, saturation point, or capacity constraint;
- an actor who can adapt to incentives, metrics, defaults, or enforcement; or
- a proxy, sample, forecast, causal claim, or missing counterfactual.

When forward analysis is active, it consumes this companion once and idea
challenge uses the resulting decision record without repeating the pass. Idea
challenge consumes it directly only when substantive planning is required and
forward analysis is inactive.

Do not activate for a typo, formatting change, exact mechanical rewrite, or an
accepted unchanged plan with no new planning decision. Inactive use adds no
file read, output field, skip receipt, lens inventory, or acknowledgement.

## The Decision Splice

Test one mechanism suggested by the live decision's structure and one credible
rival. Retain the transfer only when it changes an existing choice,
alternative, evidence request, next-consumer condition, acceptance test,
prediction, stop, or reopen trigger.

### 1. Fix the decision

Privately reduce the question to:

> Choose **A rather than B** for **this objective**, while preserving **this
> invariant**, under **these owner-controlled constraints**, for **this next
> consumer**.

Name what the next consumer must accept or do. A finished artifact that its
consumer cannot integrate, operate, verify, migrate, or recover is not the
outcome. Before assuming code is the answer, compare the no-op, an existing
path, configuration, data, documentation, or an operator sequence. Ask where
the apparent cost, toil, or risk moves and who pays when the choice fails.

Do not reopen a settled objective because another domain suggests a more
interesting one. Return proposed objective or policy changes to their owner.

### 2. Find the structure before the principle

Start from what the draft fails to account for, not from a remembered law.
Look for agents, boundaries, delays, flows, loops, signals, stocks, and
thresholds, then choose the one row that best describes the load-bearing
uncertainty. Do not traverse the router as a checklist.

Use a second mechanism only when current evidence shows that its causal
interaction with the first is itself load-bearing. Multiple interesting or
independently relevant mechanisms do not qualify.

| Signal in the decision | Mechanism to inspect | Credible rival or reversal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Requested activity may differ from accepted outcome. | Purpose, consumer acceptance, opportunity cost, and cost relocation | The requested artifact is sufficient when its next consumer accepts it and no larger recurring cost is displaced. |
| Work, requests, state, or obligations accumulate or change mode. | Arrival and drain rates, capacity, variability, feedback, delay, thresholds, and saturation | Buffering or bounded open-loop action works when bursts remain inside a measured drain window and delayed feedback would be worse. |
| Several defenses, copies, reviewers, or paths claim resilience. | Independence, common causes, reconciliation, and recovery-path dependencies | Admit another defense only for a named failure, an independent recovery path, an acceptance target, and evidence that it reduces that failure relative to a simpler existing control; otherwise simplicity wins. |
| A metric, sample, dashboard, or success report supports a broad claim. | Population, denominator, selection, counterfactuals, measurement error, and probe effects | More evidence is waste when no plausible result could change the action or collection costs more than its information value. |
| A target, default, policy, or reward can change behavior. | Agency, adaptation, proxy alignment, missing populations, incentives, and externalities | A measure is safer when diagnostic rather than rewarded, or gaming cannot separate it from the owner-defined outcome. |
| A step deletes state, closes a path, or creates lock-in. | Irreversibility, information loss, option value, carrying cost, and expiry | Commitment wins when more evidence cannot alter the choice or preserving the option now costs more than its remaining value. |
| Work or authority is divided among people, agents, teams, or services. | Coupling, serial fraction, information locality, decision rights, and each actor's signal, action, timescale, bound, shared resource or state | Before combined enablement, inventory those fields for every actor and assign one owner for the combined decision. Reverse only when a bounded perturbation shows no oscillation or load amplification and measurably faster recovery, or when proved non-overlap makes the actions independent. |
| A capability or automation can reach many users or systems. | Authority, blast radius, accountability, containment, readback, and recovery | Local autonomy wins when local actors hold better information and failures remain contained. |

### 3. Cash out the mechanism

A transfer's usable authority is the weakest link among its evidence kind, the
fidelity of the structural mapping, and the quality of current local inputs. A
formal result mapped loosely onto unmeasured variables is only a prompt. Ask
which substrate could falsify the claim: physical, formal, or social. Authority
does not cross those boundaries silently.

Map only the details that carry the conclusion:

- **Variables and units:** the requests, queues, actors, incentives, states,
  costs, permissions, populations, and compatible quantities.
- **Direction:** what increases or decreases what, and whether a loop amplifies
  or damps. A sign inversion invalidates the transfer.
- **Clock:** sensing delay, action delay, recovery time, and system-change rate.
  A mechanism acting generations later cannot govern a fleet changing in
  minutes without another mapping.
- **Boundary and scale:** components, environments, organizations, user
  populations, externalities, and included or excluded failures.
- **Agency:** who can observe, adapt, game, refuse, or bear consequences.
- **Prediction:** what becomes observable if the mapping is correct.

A compact transfer has this form:

> Under **boundary and conditions**, changing **X** should change **Y** through
> **mechanism M** over **timescale T**. Therefore alter **existing decision
> field D**. Reverse or reopen when **observable R** shows that the mechanism,
> boundary, or tradeoff no longer holds.

Run the deletion test: remove every principle, proverb, analogy name, and
domain metaphor. If the remainder cannot state concrete software variables,
mechanism, and observable evidence, it is rhetoric rather than a transfer.

Do not invent universal numeric thresholds. A number must come from an
owner-controlled policy, a formal bound whose assumptions map, current-system
measurement, or explicit local calibration whose result can change the
decision. Ordinary variation does not justify a new threshold, gate, or
process change without a baseline showing that the system changed.

### 4. Make the rival real

The rival must predict a different action in the same system. “Everything is a
tradeoff” is not opposition. State the variable it protects, the cost the first
mechanism ignores, the condition under which it wins, and the observation that
distinguishes the accounts. Do not average incompatible recommendations.

Useful discriminators include:

- evidence versus speed, decided by information value and delay harm;
- standardization versus diversity, decided by correlated failure and
  operating cost;
- prevention versus detection and recovery, decided by whether the defect
  class is enumerable;
- reversibility versus commitment, decided by option value and carrying cost;
- structural repair versus symptomatic containment, decided by recurrence and
  whether containment is reversible, time-boxed, and necessary during impact;
- open-loop versus closed-loop action, decided by whether success must be
  observed rather than assumed; and
- local autonomy versus shared control, decided by information locality,
  externalities, and containment.

### 5. Splice the result into the plan

A retained transfer leaves behind exactly three things in fields the consuming
owner already has:

1. **Decision delta:** what changed in a choice, alternative, probe, consumer
   condition, acceptance test, stop, or reopen trigger.
2. **Observable prediction:** what should happen, for whom, within which
   boundary and time.
3. **Reversal evidence:** the observed outcome that falsifies, stops, or
   reopens the recommendation.

A planned test is not itself a falsifier. State the outcome that changes the
decision. If none of the three outputs changes, discard the transfer and
continue normal planning silently.

## Epistemic Weight

Grade inflation occurs when a sound pointer is cited above the ceiling of its
kind. Fame does not raise that ceiling.

| Kind | What it can justify | What it cannot justify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Formal result under matched assumptions | Rule out an impossible combination or establish a bound | Prove that the current system satisfies the assumptions or supply missing inputs |
| Direct current-system evidence | Support a local claim within the observed population and conditions | Establish unseen states, future behavior, or causality by itself |
| Empirical pattern | Supply a prior, comparison, or focused risk check | Guarantee the same effect, magnitude, or timing elsewhere |
| Causal or systems model | Generate a mechanism, prediction, and discriminating observation | Borrow certainty without structural mapping and current evidence |
| Heuristic or proverb | Prompt a useful question or cheap precaution | Set policy, prove causality, or close disagreement |
| Metaphor | Aid recall or explanation after the mechanism is established | Serve as evidence or enter the decision by itself |
| Normative commitment | Constrain conduct when supplied by the legitimate owner | Masquerade as an empirical prediction or emerge from observed survival |

Survival, prevalence, architectural emergence, or historical persistence
describes selection pressure, switching cost, and who held decision authority;
it does not establish desirability or remove the accountable author.

## Transfer Quality Tests

Use only the tests that are load-bearing. Failure weakens or discards the
transfer; it does not create another planning ritual.

- **Units and sign:** quantities are commensurable or explicitly connected,
  and causal or feedback direction is stated, including reversal conditions.
- **Clock and scale:** the mechanism acts before the consequence and is not
  silently promoted from a request to a fleet, team, or quarter.
- **Boundary and agency:** externalities, adaptive actors, adversaries, and
  affected populations are not placed outside the model for convenience.
- **Missing population:** the denominator includes failures, filtered states,
  churned users, tails, and cohorts that could change the decision.
- **Common cause:** nominally separate controls do not share code, credentials,
  configuration, deploy paths, operators, evidence, or recovery dependencies.
- **Tampering:** a normal fluctuation does not create a new gate or adjustment
  unless current evidence distinguishes changed behavior from ordinary
  variation.
- **Counterfactual:** the proposed observation separates the mechanism from
  coincidence and from its credible rival.

## Worked Transfers

Example names are editorial and replaceable. These demonstrate the transfer
shape; they are not a catalogue or selection surface.

### Worked transfer: Recovery under saturation

- **Starting decision.** Enable retries, timeouts, scaling, and recovery actions independently because each improved an isolated test.
- **Mechanism.** The actors observe a shared symptom, add load or move shared state on different clocks, and can sustain a degraded mode after the original trigger disappears.
- **Rival.** Retries improve recovery when failures are transient and independent, spare capacity is demonstrated, repetition is safe, and attempt delay outlasts the disturbance.
- **Decision delta.** Inventory each actor's signal, action, timescale, deadline, attempt budget, and shared resource; use bounded attempts, jitter, shedding, and one coordination rule in a bounded interaction test.
- **Reversal evidence.** A perturbation shows no amplification or oscillation, faster recovery, and an observed exit from the degraded state after trigger removal.

### Worked transfer: A governed metric with a missing population

- **Starting decision.** Reward a rising throughput metric or let one successful sample authorize broad rollout.
- **Mechanism.** Adaptive actors can improve the proxy while difficult cases, failed clients, churned users, and filtered states disappear before measurement.
- **Rival.** The measure can govern when it remains causally tied to the owner-defined outcome, is difficult to game, and independent cohort evidence moves with it.
- **Decision delta.** Keep the proxy as evidence, define the full denominator, inspect excluded states and material cohorts, and add one observation that distinguishes the leading explanations.
- **Reversal evidence.** Complete-population results agree across cohorts and tails, or a newly visible population flips the broad claim despite a better average.

### Worked transfer: An expiring compatibility option

- **Starting decision.** Remove an old representation immediately or preserve it indefinitely.
- **Mechanism.** The old path retains recovery information while uncertainty remains, but dual behavior accumulates divergence, ownership, test, and incident cost.
- **Rival.** Commitment wins after consumers migrate, invariants reconcile, reconstruction no longer depends on the old path, and further evidence cannot alter the choice.
- **Decision delta.** Keep the smallest reversible path through a named evidence window with an owner, expiry, deletion conditions, and partial-failure behavior.
- **Reversal evidence.** A current consumer, unreconstructible state, or failed rollback rehearsal stops deletion; proved independence and exhausted information value permit expiry.

### Worked transfer: Parallel work that shares a spine

- **Starting decision.** Add contributors, reviewers, or recovery paths because separately named units appear independent.
- **Mechanism.** A serial semantic join and shared assumptions, mutable files, identity, configuration, operators, or recovery dependencies collapse apparent independence and add coordination cost.
- **Rival.** Parallelism or diversity wins when inputs are stable, ownership does not overlap, outputs are independently acceptable, failure paths are genuinely separate, and one owner performs the join.
- **Decision delta.** Split only mechanically joinable evidence or implementation units; keep the coupled decision with one owner and test the recovery graph rather than counting copies.
- **Reversal evidence.** Coordination consumes the expected saving, units change the same assumptions, or a bounded loss drill shows that supposedly separate paths fail together.

## Research Boundary

External research is conditional. Route it through `source-handling` only when
a current fact, disputed mechanism, exact attribution, external constraint, or
reference population can change the decision. Do not browse for a more
impressive lens. Prefer direct system evidence when it can decide the question;
otherwise mark the affected claim `Pending Verification` and name the narrow
evidence that would resolve it.

## Existing-Record Integration And Acknowledgement

Put retained effects only in fields already owned by the active decision:

- premises and evidence receive engineering claims, predictions, and
  falsifiers;
- alternatives and reopen triggers receive rejected or conditional transfers;
- next consumers receive acceptance effects and predictable blockers;
- strongest counterargument receives the credible rival; and
- claim boundary receives unproved translations as `Pending Verification`.

When forward analysis is inactive, use the matching idea-challenge fields for
strongest objection, simpler alternative, evidence, smallest safe version, and
next consumer. Do not add a `Philosophy`, `Lenses`, or `Principles applied`
field.

If a retained mechanism materially changed the rationale, the acknowledgement
is required even when forward analysis owns the output. Put exactly one plain
sentence beginning `Planning philosophy influence:` inside an existing record
field, normally `Strongest counterargument and challenge targets`, and name the
changed choice, probe, or stop rather than an inventory of names or internal
paths.

When a forward-analysis record owns the output, satisfy both output obligations
together: keep the required influence sentence inside the record, and make the
final record field the final bytes of the response. Before sending, confirm the
influence sentence is present when the mechanism changed the decision, then
delete every decision, rationale, summary, acknowledgement, or closing sentence
after `Invalidation and reopen conditions:`. Do not satisfy the terminal-record
rule by dropping a required influence sentence, or satisfy the influence rule
by appending a recap. When forward analysis is inactive, keep the acknowledgement
inside an existing rationale field.

If no decision changed, do not claim the philosophy was followed, embodied,
validated, or influential. Continue with the normal planning result without an
acknowledgement.

## Failure Modes And Stop Rule

Stop and revise when a label cannot survive deletion, a rival is harmless, a
causal or current premise is unverified, a transfer would change an
owner-controlled field, or added analysis cannot change the decision.

Stop the chamber after one structurally sound mechanism and its credible rival
have changed the decision or failed to do so. When current evidence proves a
causal interaction, test that coupled mechanism and no unrelated second lens.
More principles do not create more confidence.

The chamber succeeds only when the planner will choose, test, stop, or reopen
differently for a stated engineering reason.
